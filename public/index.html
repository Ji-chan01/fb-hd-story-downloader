How this Works?

1. Copy profile link

Find the public Facebook profile or page you want to view stories from, and copy its URL/link from the browser address bar.

2. Paste profile link here

Paste the copied URL/link into the search box on IncognitoFB, then click the SEARCH button to load the user's active stories.

3. Review or Save the File!

Explore the available public stories. Click on any story thumbnail to open it in full view, select your preferred video resolution or audio settings, and download the high-resolution media anonymously.
